# **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord Automatic DLC Unlocker & Activator**

A simple tool that automatically downloads, unlocks and activates DLCs in **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord**.

---

## 🔗 Latest Release of Bannerlord DLC Unlocker

- **💾 Version 5.0.0.8**
  👉 [Latest Release]()

---

### Supported DLCs:

- Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord - War Sails
- Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord - Digital Companion
- Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord Original Soundtrack

---

## 🧰 **Dependencies:**

- A **Steam version** of **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord**
- The **latest release** of **CreamAPI**
- An internet connection (for SteamCMD data fetch)
- **.NET 7 Runtime** installed (if not already)

---

## 🚀 **Activation Guide**

### Get CreamAPI and follow the instructions
- Download the latest release of **CreamAPI**.
- Extract the archive to any location (it’s fully portable).
- Launch **CreamAPI** and follow the setup instructions to activate DLCs for **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord**.

---

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting**

| Problem                        | Solution                                                                 |
|---------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| DLCs not showing in-game        | Ensure CreamAPI was run as Administrator and restart Steam.              |
| Game crashes after launch       | Use the “Uninstall” option in CreamAPI and reinstall unlockers.         |
| Missing DLL errors              | Reinstall Koaloader via CreamInstaller’s context menu.                   |
| Still doesn’t work              | Re-run CreamAPI and refresh Steam appinfo for Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord.    |

---

## 🧠 **Tips & Notes**

- **Back up your Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord folder** before making changes.
- **SteamCMD** will download automatically if missing.
- Works with **official Steam builds only**.
- If **Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord** updates, simply rerun **CreamAPI** to reapply the unlockers.
